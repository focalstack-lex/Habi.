### Lens 1: Intellectual Property & Trademark Protection

#### [P2] Unattributed Third-Party Trademark Mentions in Catalog Data
- **Location**: [`src/data/mockProducts.ts:L46`](file:///c:/Users/User/Pictures/Habi/src/data/mockProducts.ts#L46), [`src/data/mockProducts.ts:L96`](file:///c:/Users/User/Pictures/Habi/src/data/mockProducts.ts#L96)

### Lens 2: Website Accessibility (ADA Title III & WCAG 2.1 AA)

#### [P2] Empty Alt Attribute on Product Detail Gallery Thumbnails
- **Location**: [`src/components/product/ProductDetailModal.tsx:L160`](file:///c:/Users/User/Pictures/Habi/src/components/product/ProductDetailModal.tsx#L160)
- **Evidence**: `<img src={img} alt="" className="w-full h-full object-cover" />` contains an empty `alt=""` string for informative product gallery thumbnails.
- **Legal Risk**: Screen reader users cannot identify or distinguish selectable image thumbnails, violating WCAG 2.1 AA Success Criterion 1.1.1 (Non-text Content).
- **Proposed Remediation**: Provide descriptive alt strings such as `alt={`${product.name} thumbnail ${index + 1}`}`.

#### [P2] Missing Form Input Accessible Labels in Navigation Header
- **Location**: [`src/components/layout/NavigationHeader.tsx:L80`](file:///c:/Users/User/Pictures/Habi/src/components/layout/NavigationHeader.tsx#L80)
- **Evidence**: `<input type="text" placeholder="Search pieces, brands, hoodies..." ... />` lacks an associated `<label>` or `aria-label`.
- **Legal Risk**: Screen readers fail to announce input purpose to visually impaired users, violating WCAG 2.1 AA Success Criterion 1.3.1 (Info and Relationships) and 4.1.2 (Name, Role, Value).
- **Proposed Remediation**: Add `aria-label="Search local Davao fashion pieces and sellers"` to the input element.

#### [P3] Unlabeled Interactive Action Buttons
- **Location**: [`src/components/feed/ProductCard.tsx:L45`](file:///c:/Users/User/Pictures/Habi/src/components/feed/ProductCard.tsx#L45), [`src/components/community/FitCheckCard.tsx:L35`](file:///c:/Users/User/Pictures/Habi/src/components/community/FitCheckCard.tsx#L35)
- **Evidence**: Bookmark and like icon buttons lack `aria-label` descriptors.
- **Legal Risk**: Assistive technology reads icon buttons as unlabelled button controls.
- **Proposed Remediation**: Add explicit `aria-label="Save product"` and `aria-label="Like post"` properties.

### Lens 3: Data Privacy, Cybersecurity & Cookies (GDPR / CCPA)

#### [P1] Missing Mandatory Privacy Policy & Terms of Service Routes
- **Location**: [`src/components/layout/FooterSection.tsx:L99-L106`](file:///c:/Users/User/Pictures/Habi/src/components/layout/FooterSection.tsx#L99-L106)
- **Evidence**: Site footer and navigation drawers contain no links or modal triggers for Privacy Policy (`/privacy`) or Terms of Service (`/terms`).
- **Legal Risk**: Violation of statutory consumer transparency laws (California Online Privacy Protection Act, GDPR Article 13, and Philippine Data Privacy Act of 2012) requiring accessible disclosures regarding user data collection, storage, and seller interaction terms.
- **Proposed Remediation**: Implement a dedicated legal modal / route footer drawer with complete Privacy Policy and Terms of Service disclosures.

### Lens 4: E-Commerce & Consumer Protection Laws

#### [P2] Absence of Explicit Return, Exchange & Final Sale Terms
- **Location**: [`src/components/product/ProductDetailModal.tsx:L250`](file:///c:/Users/User/Pictures/Habi/src/components/product/ProductDetailModal.tsx#L250), [`src/components/product/InstantInquiryModal.tsx:L85`](file:///c:/Users/User/Pictures/Habi/src/components/product/InstantInquiryModal.tsx#L85)
- **Evidence**: Product detail and inquiry modals list price and condition but omit explicit return/exchange policy disclaimers for 1-of-1 vintage thrift items.
- **Legal Risk**: E-commerce protection regulations mandate clear pre-purchase disclosures regarding seller return eligibility, refunds, and final-sale terms.
- **Proposed Remediation**: Include a standard pre-purchase disclosure tag ("1-of-1 vintage items are final sale; inspect condition grading prior to seller inquiry").

### Lens 5: Product Liability & Safety Advisories

#### [P3] Missing Pre-Owned Garment Care & Sanitization Disclaimer

### Lens 7: Tax & Financial Transparency

#### [P2] Missing Regional Tax & Payment Method Disclosures
- **Location**: [`src/components/product/InstantInquiryModal.tsx:L120`](file:///c:/Users/User/Pictures/Habi/src/components/product/InstantInquiryModal.tsx#L120)
